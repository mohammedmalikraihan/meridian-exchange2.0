Our Approach

The 2026 cloud infrastructure disruption served as a global wake-up call, exposing the fragile "policy trap" where national security remains tethered to external digital dependencies. Reliance without resilience is no longer an option.

The Meridian Exchange introduces the Supply Chain Security Doctrine. We move beyond simple procurement toward a framework of digital sovereignty, ensuring that the critical components of the modern state—from chips to clouds—are verifiable, secure, and sovereign.
